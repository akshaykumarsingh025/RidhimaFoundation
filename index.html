Who We Are

Because Every Woman Deserves to be Heard, Healed, and Honored.

The Ridhima Foundation is more than a healthcare initiative; it is a movement to bridge the gap between medical need and accessible care. Founded by Dr. Deepika Singh, we are dedicated to the physical, emotional, and social well-being of women—specifically the underserved, the invisible, and the forgotten.

In a world where women often put their families first and their health last, we step in to ensure they are prioritized.

Founder's Message

"In my years of practice, I have seen women endure unimaginable pain silently—sacrificing their health to save money for their children, their husbands, or their households. I have seen women ashamed to ask for help.

Ridhima Foundation is my promise to them. It is a promise that you are not alone. It is a promise that your pain matters. We are here to hold your hand, heal your body, and restore your dignity."

— Dr. Deepika Singh

Core Impact Programs

Comprehensive healthcare solutions tailored for women

🌷 Project Ridhima Outreach

Bringing top-tier gynecology and fertility consultations to the doorsteps of urban slums and rural belts. We go where the hospitals can't reach.

🌸 Hope for Motherhood

Financial status should not dictate fertility. We provide subsidized IVF, IUI, scans, and medicines for couples battling infertility and financial stress.

🌼 Kishori Shakti

A school-based program focusing on menstrual hygiene, anemia screening, and nutrition counseling to build a generation of strong girls.

🎗 The Pink Shield

Free mammograms, pap smears, and vaccination drives. We fight cancer through awareness and early detection.

🌺 Inner Healing

Health is not just physical. We offer counseling for marital stress, infertility trauma, postpartum depression, and anxiety.

🌹 Graceful Aging

Dedicated clinics for bone density tests, hormonal counseling, and lifestyle guidance for women entering their second innings.
