01 — Who we are

Because every woman deserves to be heard, healed, and honored.

The Ridhima Foundation is more than a healthcare initiative; it is a movement to bridge the gap between medical need and accessible care. Founded by Dr. Deepika Singh alongside co-founder Dr. Raj Kumar Singh, we are dedicated to the physical, emotional, and social well-being of women—specifically the underserved, the invisible, and the forgotten.

In a world where women often put their families first and their health last, we step in to ensure they are prioritized.

Healthcare is a right, not a privilege.

Founder

Dr. Deepika Singh

MBBS, MD (AIIMS New Delhi) · MCCG, Germany

Obstetrician & Gynaecologist

An obstetrician and gynaecologist trained at AIIMS New Delhi, Dr. Deepika Singh has spent her career caring for women through pregnancy, fertility, surgery and menopause. Ridhima Foundation grew out of what she saw in her own practice — women delaying treatment they could not afford, and suffering in silence for want of someone to ask.

“We believe that good health is much more than just being disease-free — and hence, rather than just treating the disease, we focus on healing the person.”

      Questions & answers

      Frequently asked questions.

      Everything you need to know about our work and how to donate. Still have a question? Write to us.

      What is Ridhima Foundation?

      Ridhima Foundation is a women's health NGO in New Delhi, founded by AIIMS-trained gynaecologist Dr. Deepika Singh with co-founder Dr. Raj Kumar Singh. We run free medical camps, cancer screening, adolescent and menstrual health programmes, menopause care and subsidised fertility treatment for underserved women across Delhi NCR.

      How can I donate to Ridhima Foundation?

      Scan the UPI QR code on this page with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or any UPI app, or pay to the UPI ID ridhimafoundati834073.rzp@rxairtel. You can give any amount, and payments are processed securely by Razorpay.

      Is my donation tax-deductible under Section 80G?

      Yes. Ridhima Foundation is registered under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act, so your donation is eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G. After donating, send your name, PAN, address and UPI transaction ID to us on WhatsApp at +91 8595954095 to receive your 80G receipt.

      How is my donation used?

      Every rupee donated goes directly toward patient care and camp logistics — free medicines, cancer screenings, menstrual hygiene kits, medical camps and subsidised fertility treatment for women who cannot afford them.

      Are the medical camps really free?

      Yes. Consultations, medicines and health screenings at our camps are completely free, with no hidden charges. Our camps reach urban slums and rural areas where hospitals are hard to reach.

      Where does Ridhima Foundation work?

      We work across Delhi NCR, holding camps in urban slums and rural belts. Our office is at F-11, South Extension Part 1, New Delhi.

      How can I volunteer or partner with you for CSR?

      Doctors, nurses, counsellors and volunteers are always welcome, and companies can adopt a medical camp or sponsor treatments as part of their CSR. Fill in the contact form on this page or message us on WhatsApp at +91 8595954095.

      Do you offer free health workshops?

      Yes. Dr. Deepika Singh hosts free live workshops and recorded videos on our YouTube channel, in Hindi and English, covering menstrual health, PCOS, pregnancy, breast self-examination and menopause.
