🩺 WHO WE ARE
Because Every Woman Deserves to be Heard, Healed, and Honored.
The Ridhima Foundation is more than a healthcare initiative; it is a movement to bridge the gap between medical need and accessible care. Founded by Dr. Deepika Singh, we are dedicated to the physical, emotional, and social well-being of women—specifically the underserved, the invisible, and the forgotten.

In a world where women often put their families first and their health last, we step in to ensure they are prioritized.

🌟 OUR HOLISTIC COMMITMENT
A Lifetime of Care: From the Girl Child to the Golden Years
You asked to emphasize our care for girls and women. At Ridhima Foundation, we don’t just treat symptoms; we nurture lives through a specific "Circle of Care":

👧 1. Nurturing the Girl Child (Adolescence & Puberty)
We believe that a healthy girl becomes an empowered woman.

Menstrual Dignity: Breaking taboos, providing eco-friendly hygiene kits, and teaching menstrual management.

Anemia & Nutrition Watch: Combatting the "silent killer" of anemia in teenage girls to ensure physical stamina and focus in school.

PCOS & Hormonal Harmony: Early detection and lifestyle guidance for young girls suffering from PCOD/PCOS, acne, and weight issues.

HPV Vaccination Awareness: Educating families about the cervical cancer vaccine to protect the future generation.

👩 2. Supporting the Woman (Reproductive Years)
We stand by women during their most demanding years.

Safe Motherhood: Pre-natal care, nutrition for expecting mothers, and post-partum recovery support.

The Right to Fertility: Ensuring that financial constraints do not deny a couple the joy of parenthood (Subsidized IVF/IUI).

Intimate Hygiene & UTI Prevention: Education on preventing common infections that are often ignored due to shame.

👵 3. Honoring the Matriarch (Menopause & Senior Care)
We ensure women age with grace and strength.

Bone Health & Mobility: Screening for Osteoporosis and Calcium deficiency to prevent fractures in older age.

Menopause Management: Counseling for hot flashes, mood swings, and hormonal shifts so women don't suffer in silence.

Cancer Shield: Aggressive screening for Breast and Cervical cancer—because early detection saves lives.

👁️ OUR VISION & MISSION
Our Vision: To build an India where healthcare is a fundamental right, not a privilege. A future where no woman suffers in silence, and where healthcare is accessible, respectful, and emotionally supportive.

Our Mission:

Provide free and heavily subsidized medical interventions.

Educate communities to break stigmas around infertility, menstruation, and mental health.

Heal the body through medicine and the mind through counseling.

Empower women to take charge of their own health independence.

🚀 CORE IMPACT PROGRAMS
🌷 1. Project Ridhima Outreach (OPD Camps)
Bringing top-tier gynecology and fertility consultations to the doorsteps of urban slums and rural belts. We go where the hospitals can't reach.

🌸 2. The "Hope for Motherhood" Initiative
Financial status should not dictate fertility. We provide subsidized IVF, IUI, scans, and medicines for couples battling infertility and financial stress.

🌼 3. "Kishori Shakti" (Adolescent Health)
A school-based program focusing on menstrual hygiene, anemia screening, and nutrition counseling to build a generation of strong girls.

🎗 4. The Pink Shield (Cancer Defense)
Free mammograms, pap smears, and vaccination drives. We fight cancer through awareness and early detection.

🌺 5. Inner Healing (Mental Wellness)
Health is not just physical. We offer counseling for marital stress, infertility trauma, postpartum depression, and anxiety.

🌹 6. Graceful Aging (Menopause Care)
Dedicated clinics for bone density tests, hormonal counseling, and lifestyle guidance for women entering their second innings.
